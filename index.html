Your board, your way ✨

Write: choose Pen or Highlight. Eraser removes the stroke you touch. Undo brings it back.

Move: choose Select, tap an item, then drag its label or picture. Drag the blue corner to resize. Double-click text to edit. In Select mode, drag a box around several pen strokes to move or resize a whole handwritten character. Click a selected item’s Delete button to remove it.

Practice: Board → Three-line writing. Letters has uppercase and lowercase models you can place on the board.

Pages: + Page keeps earlier work. Use page tabs to return, or ⧉ to make a copy.

Tools: open a calculator, poll, clocks, timers and more. Drag their headings, resize their corners, or use ⤢ to enlarge.

Keep your lesson: Save board downloads all pages, uploaded media, fonts and tools. Open loads it again. Your browser also keeps an automatic local copy when storage is available.
